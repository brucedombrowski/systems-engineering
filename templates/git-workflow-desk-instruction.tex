\textbf{Purpose:} This one-page instruction explains how to review, comment on, and approve changes using your web browser. No software installation required.

\section*{The 5-Step Review Workflow}

\begin{center}
\fbox{\textbf{1. Open URL}} $\rightarrow$
\fbox{\textbf{2. Browse Files}} $\rightarrow$
\fbox{\textbf{3. View Changes}} $\rightarrow$
\fbox{\textbf{4. Comment}} $\rightarrow$
\fbox{\textbf{5. Approve}}
\end{center}

\section*{Step-by-Step Instructions}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{Open the project URL} in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0pt]
        \item You will receive the URL by email or message from the project team.
        \item Bookmark it for easy access.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Browse files.} The project landing page shows all files. Click any file to see its contents. Click folders to navigate.

    \item \textbf{View changes.} When someone proposes a change:
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=0pt]
        \item You will receive an email notification with a link.
        \item Click the link to open the \textbf{Merge Request} (GitLab) or \textbf{Pull Request} (GitHub).
        \item Click the \textbf{``Changes''} tab to see the diff.
        \item \textbf{Choose your view:} \textit{Inline} shows old and new together; \textit{Side-by-side} shows old on the left, new on the right. Toggle this with the button at the top of the Changes tab. Side-by-side is often easier to read for large changes.
        \item \textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{Green lines}} = text that was \textbf{added}.
        \item \textcolor{red!70!black}{\textbf{Red lines}} = text that was \textbf{removed}.
        \item Unchanged text is shown in gray for context.
    \end{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Leave a comment.} Click on any line in the diff to leave a comment. Ask questions, request changes, or note concerns. All comments are saved permanently.

    \item \textbf{Approve.} When you are satisfied with the changes, click the \textbf{``Approve''} button. Once all required reviewers approve, the changes are merged into the official version.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Viewing History}

To see previous versions of any file:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, itemsep=2pt]
    \item Open the file in your browser.
    \item Click \textbf{``History''} (GitHub) or \textbf{``History''} (GitLab) in the top-right area.
    \item Each entry shows: \textit{who} changed it, \textit{when}, and a \textit{summary} of what changed.
    \item Click any entry to see the exact changes made at that point.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Key Rules (Enforced Automatically)}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lX}
\toprule
\textbf{Rule} & \textbf{What it means} \\
\midrule
No direct changes to main & All changes must go through a Merge/Pull Request \\
Required approvers & Changes cannot be merged until designated reviewers approve \\
Author cannot self-approve & The person who made the change cannot approve their own change \\
Stale approval reset & If changes are made after approval, reviewers must re-approve \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
